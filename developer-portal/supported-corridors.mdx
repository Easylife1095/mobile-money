## KYC Tier Limits

To comply with local regulations and Stellar's SEP-12 standard, transaction limits are also constrained by the user's KYC tier.

| Tier | Requirements | Transaction Limit (USD Eqv) | Daily Limit (USD Eqv) |
|------|--------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------|
| Tier 0 | Phone Number only | $50 | $200 |
| Tier 1 | Phone + Name + DOB | $500 | $2,000 |
| Tier 2 | ID Document + Selfie | $5,000 | $20,000 |
| Tier 3 | Proof of Address + Source of Funds | Custom | Custom |

*Note: USD Equivalent limits are approximate and dynamically calculated based on real-time Oracle price feeds on the Stellar network.*
