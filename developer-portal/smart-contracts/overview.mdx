Mobile Money utilizes Soroban smart contracts on the Stellar network to ensure secure, trustless, and atomic operations.

## Why Soroban?
Soroban is a developer-friendly, Rust-based smart contract platform that natively integrates with Stellar's high-throughput network, providing low fees and fast finality.
