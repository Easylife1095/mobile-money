Hash Time-Locked Contracts (HTLCs) enable trustless atomic swaps between Mobile Money agents and Stellar users.

## Mechanism
1. **Hashlock**: Funds are locked with a cryptographic hash. The exact preimage is required to unlock them.
2. **Timelock**: If the preimage is not revealed before the expiry time, funds are safely returned to the sender.

This ensures neither party can run away with funds midway through a swap.
