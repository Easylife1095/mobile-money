## What is Mobile Money?

Mobile Money is an enterprise-grade API platform that connects over 500 million African mobile money wallets to the Stellar network. We enable seamless cross-border payments, remittances, and B2B flows by tokenizing local fiat (XAF, XOF, KES, etc.) into fiat-backed tokens on Stellar, facilitating instant settlement globally.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit (Mobile Money In)">
    Users initiate a deposit from their local mobile money wallet (e.g., MTN MoMo). The funds are received by our local treasury accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer (On-Chain)">
    An equivalent amount of fiat-backed tokens is minted or transferred on the Stellar blockchain, settling in seconds with near-zero fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw (Mobile Money Out)">
    Tokens are burned or held in escrow while the destination local currency is instantly paid out to the recipient's mobile money wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Built on Stellar

Our infrastructure is deeply integrated with the Stellar ecosystem to ensure compliance, transparency, and speed:

- **SEP Compliance:** Fully implements SEP-10 (Authentication), SEP-12 (KYC), SEP-24 (Interactive Asset Issuance), SEP-31 (Cross-Border Payments), and SEP-38 (Quotes).
- **Soroban Smart Contracts:** Leveraging Soroban for advanced escrow, HTLC (Hash Time Locked Contracts), oracle price feeds, and automated vault liquidity management.
- **Horizon API:** Direct integration with Stellar's Horizon nodes for robust transaction submission and monitoring.
